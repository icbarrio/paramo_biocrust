# Paramo biocrust

## Contact info: Alejandro Salazar (alejandro@lbhi.is) and Isabel Barrio (isabel@lbhi.is)

The repository contains data on surface cover (biocrust, moss, lichen, vascular plants bare ground and rocks), soil surface roughness and stability across a glacier forefield chronosequence at the Nevados National Park, Colombia. 

*Reference of pre-print at Research square:* Salazar, A., Gutiérrez-Lagoueyte, M. E., Jónsdóttir, I. S., Barrio, I. C., Guðmundsdóttir, H. I., Rodriguez-Caballero, E., & Sanchez-Andrade, A. (2026). Biocrust belt below the edge of a dying glacier in the Tropical Andes. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-9044279/v1. (Manuscript currently under review at Alpine Botany)
